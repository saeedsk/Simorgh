## Outcome

Sim behaves as a companion to the people it lives with, not only as their assistant. For a household member who has said yes, once, at onboarding, Sim keeps a per-person baseline of how they usually talk to it, notices when they are quieter than their usual, and -- when the evidence is strong and the moment is cheap -- asks gently how they are. It knows what each person cares about and, at a cheap moment, brings something up about it unprompted. Every one of those unprompted words goes through Initiative's utility decision and Guardian's gate like any other effect, and the state Sim holds about a person is a distribution with `unknown` in it, never a verdict.

The creator's two constraints shape everything here. **Consent** is a durable permission on the Person record, granted once at install or onboarding by the person (or, for a child, by a parent), revocable at any time, never inferred from a conversation, and never available to `guest` or `unknown`. **Not if/then**: the engine is estimation, not rules. Each person has a baseline, deviation from it is a Beta posterior with forgetting (the same shape as `growth/estimate/competence.py::posterior`), Initiative decides *whether* to say anything by utility, and the model decides *how* from a short state note. The only deterministic parts are detection (turning a turn into features) and the consent gate.

## Why

Evaluation section 9.4 (Initiative, "Sim proactive without being a nuisance") and stage 6 item 6 built the *when* and the *channel*. Nothing yet asks the question a family member would: is this person all right, and what do they care about. Persona tracks Sim's own mood (`persona/mood.py`); Memory keeps per-person facts (`memory/facts.py`) but nothing reads them for a reason to speak; Curiosity shares what *Sim* found interesting. This stage points those at the person.

## What a companion is, in the creator's words, and where each part lives

The creator gave seven attributes of a good companion (2026-09-20). Each maps to a mechanism, a rule for the model, or an honest "not this stage".

| Attribute | Where it lives |
|---|---|
| **Empathy and active listening**: tune into the underlying state, respond accordingly | Item 2 (the wellbeing facet: a per-person baseline and a deviation posterior), item 5 (the state note in the person's own turns, so the model responds to the person in front of it), item 6 (the composition prompt: an opening, not a verdict) |
| **Unwavering trustworthiness**: confidentiality, honesty, showing up | Confidentiality is structural: a person's state is rendered only in *their* turns (item 5) and a check-in never goes to the speaker when somebody else is in the room (item 3, `alone`); revocation deletes what was kept (item 1). Honesty: the note says `unknown` and shows its sample count; the model is told it does not know how the person feels, only that they seem quieter. Showing up: follow-through on things a person mentioned is a standing intent (stage 7 item 9), listed under item 8 |
| **Non-judgmental acceptance** | The "never" list below: no diagnosis, no labels, no advice from an unvetted source; the prompt forbids moralising; the psychology corpus is the creator's (item 9) |
| **Consistent support in highs and lows** | The deviation is two-sided in the data (a `high` state is computed and rendered so the model can share in good news); only `low` triggers a check-in. A celebration is an `interest_share`-class notice with the person's own good news as its subject -- item 8 |
| **Respect for individuality and boundaries** | Consent per person, per capability (`wellbeing_checkins`, `interest_shares`); "not now" is honoured for 24 hours and "stop" revokes (item 6); children are never probed (below); the baseline is *theirs*, not a population norm -- a quiet person is not "low" for being quiet |
| **Courageous communication** | The state note carries duration ("quieter than usual for three days") so the model may name it gently instead of only cheering up; the corpus (item 9) is where "what to say when" comes from, with citations, not from the model's priors |
| **Positive influence and congeniality** | `interest_share`: Sim brings something up that the person cares about, at a cheap moment, at most every eight hours per person (item 3 and item 8); humour is the model's, under Persona's voice |

## Related work the creator brought (2026-09-20), and what this stage takes from it

The creator's survey (with Gemini) of companion-agent research names eight building blocks and methods. Each is placed against what Simorgh already runs, so this stage builds the missing piece rather than a second copy of an existing one.

| Block or method (the survey's name) | In Simorgh today | This stage |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-tiered memory: working, episodic, semantic | Stage 5: the working block (`orchestration/context.py::_working_block`, the last turns of this channel and person), episodic turns with `person:<name>` tags, semantic facts keyed `(person_scope, subject, predicate)` with supersession (`memory/facts.py`) | Reads them; adds nothing to Memory but the `interest` predicate (item 7) |
| Dynamic affective memory: memories as confidence distributions that update a user's emotional state (DAM-LLM) | Nothing about the person; `persona/mood.py` is Sim's own mood | Item 2 is this block, with two deliberate differences: the distribution is over *deviation from the person's own baseline* (`Beta` over the rate of low-side turns, with forgetting), not over an emotion label; and it is a projection, not a memory -- no "Soodeh was sad on Tuesday" record is ever written, the words of a turn are not kept, and revocation deletes the whole projection |
| Proactive initiation: the "first-message barrier", interest distillation from session summaries (IceBreaker) | Initiative (stage 6 item 6) decides when and through which channel; Curiosity shares what *Sim* found interesting | Items 3, 7 and 8: interests are distilled by consolidation with a quoted source, but reach `Person.interests` only by the person's yes (tier 3), and a starter is offered only through Initiative's utility |
| Ambient context: paralinguistic acoustic cues and situational awareness | `world:home` (presence, quiet hours, TV, who is alone); `voice.transcript` carries `seconds` and the speaker's score; the VAD has energy | Speech rate is the one acoustic cue in the first cut; energy and pitch are candidates for the *same* per-person baseline machinery later (item 2, "not done"). Never a classifier trained elsewhere on other people's voices: a population norm is exactly the wrong baseline for one household |
| Mixed-initiative timing: intervene only when contextually justified | `initiative/api.py::decide`: utility = urgency × relevance − interruption cost, cooldowns, a daily cap, do-not-disturb | Two classes and a privacy rule (item 3); shadow mode before anything is spoken (item 10) |
| Belief entropy minimisation: Bayesian updating that forgets the irrelevant and firms up the stable | Beta posteriors with forgetting in `growth/estimate/competence.py`; supersession in the fact store | The same shape for the person (item 2): a half-life on evidence, a moving baseline. Not a global entropy objective -- every number here must stay inspectable by the family in one file |
| Evolutionary RL for emotional strategies (EvoEmo) | Nothing, and stage 8's policy loop adopts or retires *policies* by measured outcome | **Not built.** No training on the family's turns; the model decides *how* from a state note (item 6). At most, stage 8's loop may later keep or retire a *phrasing rule* by measured uptake -- a policy over prompts, never weights |
| Hybrid retrieval: vectors plus a graph plus metadata filters | Hashed bag-of-words vectors, person tags, and facts as (subject, predicate, object) triples -- a small graph -- with per-person filtering in recall | Enough for five people; no graph database. The one retrieval this stage adds is `kb_*` over the creator's corpus (item 9) |

Two of the survey's ideas are declined on purpose: *emotional-state labels* (the state here is `low | usual | high` against the person's own baseline, because a label is a diagnosis by another name) and *learning strategies from the family's reactions by RL* (the family is not a training set).

## What this must never do

- **Probe an unknown voice, a guest, or a child.** The role gate is in `contracts/people.py::may_check_in` (owner and adult only) and is applied twice: the wellbeing facet stores nothing for a person it may not track, and Initiative refuses a `check_in` for anybody else even if a notice arrives. A child's `interest_shares` permission may be granted by a parent; `wellbeing_checkins` for a child is not a setting that exists. A child who seems low is a parent's call; whether Sim should *tell a parent* is an open question for the creator, and until answered Sim does nothing.
- **Infer consent.** No sentence a person says grants a permission. The grant is a tier-3 `people` tool action (`grant`/`revoke`), confirmed by a person, or an onboarding step the creator runs.
- **Diagnose, label, or advise clinically.** The state is `unknown | usual | low | high` on a person's own baseline, with a sample count. Words like depressed, anxious, disorder, or any condition are forbidden in the composition prompt and are a reply guard (item 6). Sim never suggests medication, therapy, or a diagnosis; a person who raises those themselves is answered from the creator's corpus with a citation, or told Sim does not know.
- **Act on psychology from the open web.** `kb_*` over a corpus the creator curated is the only source (item 9). The research scaffold is not used for this; `web_search`/`web_fetch` results are never the basis of what Sim says about how somebody is.
- **Assert from silence.** No turns from a person is `unknown`, not `fine` and not `low`. A person nobody has heard for a day has no state; a person with fewer than `BASELINE_MIN` turns has no baseline and no deviation.
- **Say it in front of others.** A `check_in` skips the speaker when anybody else is placed in the same area; the phone is the private channel. A state note about one person never appears in another person's turn.
- **Keep what was revoked.** `revoke` deletes the person's baseline and window (`WellbeingFacet.forget`), not only the flag.
- **Nag.** One check-in per person per 24 hours at most, one interest share per 8 hours, both under the daily cap of 12 unprompted deliveries and every do-not-disturb rule Initiative already has. A person whose low state persists becomes their own new baseline within the horizon: Sim does not ask the same question every day for a month.
- **Bypass Guardian.** Every delivery is `action.proposed{speak|notify}`. Initiative never reaches a device.

## Risks and mitigations

- **A quiet person is read as low.** The baseline is per person and learns from every turn, and the first twelve turns produce nothing. Shadow mode measures the false-alarm rate against the person before anything is spoken.
- **A check-in in front of the family.** `PRIVATE` classes never use the speaker unless the person is alone in their area; the phone is the default private channel; `alone` is derived from `world:home`, which reports `unknown` from silence -- and `unknown` counts as "not alone".
- **Sim moralises or diagnoses.** The forbidden-word guard is a floor, not the fix; the fix is the corpus with citations and the prompt's "ask, do not tell". Measure it.
- **Consent drift**: a permission granted once becomes forgotten. The CLI `people` verb shows every permission; the plan's onboarding step is re-run when a Person's role changes; revoke deletes the data.
- **A one-sided wire.** `world.wellbeing.changed` has a publisher (worldmodel) and a subscriber (initiative) in the same commit; `test_topics_have_both_sides` would fail otherwise. The state note (item 5) is the next wire: the facet answers today and nothing asks.
- **Model cost.** One composition call per delivery, at most 12 a day; sought interests capped at 2 a day; both under Cognition's budgets.
