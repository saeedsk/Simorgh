# The disk crossed 5% free, and two boot tests went red (2026-09-20)

## What happened

`tests/simorgh/integration/test_kernel_boot_two_toy_subsystems.py` and
`test_worldmodel_persona_interface_flow.py` began failing with

    AssertionError: 'degraded' != 'ok'

for the Ledger, in the middle of an otherwise clean run. Nothing in the
Ledger had changed. The volume had 4.7% free, and
`ledger/service.py::health` reports `degraded` under `LOW_DISK_FRACTION`
(5%) -- which is exactly what it is for.

Both tests asserted `status == "ok"` at boot. That is an assertion about
the machine. To anyone who saw the run it read as "the Kernel is broken",
which is the worst thing a false red can say.

They now use `tests/simorgh/integration/assert_up`: `ok`, or `degraded`
for a reason whose detail names the disk. Anything else degraded, and
anything `down`, still fails -- those are the states the assertion exists
for.

## Where the space actually is

| Path | Size | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `/var/folders/.../T/simorgh-*` (abandoned trial labs, 19 Sep) | 30 GB apparent, **2 GB real** | deleted |
| `workspace/voice/models` + `venvs` | 6.4 GB | earns its keep: Kokoro, whisper, the sherpa embedder and their environments |
| `workspace/cameras` | 837 MB | snapshots and clips |
| `workspace/tv` | 516 MB | cast media |
| `workspace/swebench` | 434 MB | benchmark checkouts |
| `workspace/voice/audio` (kept turns) | 41 MB | the retention policy is working (`keep_audio_days` 7, `keep_audio_max_mb` 500) |

The abandoned labs measured 30 GB and freed 2 GB, which is the APFS
clone working as designed: `fast_copy_repo` uses `cp -c`, so a lab's
blocks are shared with the repo until something writes to them, and `du`
reports the apparent size. The 2026-09-14 finding about `copytree`
filling the disk does NOT apply to these -- the fast path is doing its
job, and the honest number for a stale lab is closer to a few hundred
megabytes.

Which means the real pressure is not Simorgh's garbage. The volume is
460 GB with about 25 GB free; Simorgh's whole working set is 8.2 GB and
three quarters of that is models it needs to hear and speak. **Anything
that wants room -- the 1.5 GB Home Assistant image, for instance --
needs space cleared somewhere else on this machine.**

## What is worth doing next

- Stale labs are never cleaned up when a trial is killed rather than
  finishing (`tools/trial.py` deletes its own root in a `finally`, which
  a `kill -9` skips). A sweep of `simorgh-*` temp directories older than
  a day, run at boot, would keep this from being noticed by a red test
  again.
- `LOW_DISK_FRACTION` at 5% of a 460 GB volume is 23 GB, which is a lot
  of headroom to call "low". A floor in gigabytes as well as a fraction
  would fit both a laptop and a Pi; the fraction alone does not.
